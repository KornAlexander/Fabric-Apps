🎮 The apps I build (1/13): Fabric Empires

Fabric Apps are crazy. The sky is the limit. Don't believe me? Check this out.

I turned the official Microsoft exam topics into a data product. It just happens to look like a strategy game. Finally a learning path I actually want to go on.

▸ The exam topics ARE the tech tree. Every battle asks you a question. Right answer, you win the fight. Wrong answer, you lose ground.
▸ Bring your own topics. Swap the spreadsheet and it builds a tech tree out of your question bank.
▸ Run it in Fabric and every answer lands in a Fabric SQL database, ready to analyse in Power BI.

Will your kid take the second seat and push you to try harder? Will you conquer the map together?

Mine does. Ben answers his own elementary math questions while I answer Direct Lake stuff, and the game averages the two of us.

Free, open to reuse, runs in the browser.

Personal project. NOT MICROSOFT AFFILIATED.

Play it, free and in the browser:
