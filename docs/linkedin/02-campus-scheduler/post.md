Every semester starts with the same planning questions. The answers live in Excel, in mails, and in a few people's heads.

Campus Twin turns that into a model of the real campus, on ground that has been officially surveyed. Every room, every capacity.

Then Tuesday comes. Eight in the morning, a lecture hall falls out, and the lecturer is off sick too.

You ask in an ordinary sentence. You see which sessions are hit and how many students. The plan is worked out again in front of you, and you see how the answer came about.

A proposal, not a decision.

Whether it is a timetable or a staffing plan, we fit it to what you actually need.

A few days ago this was one university's question. Your idea, our products, our team. Tell us what you need and we will build the demo.
