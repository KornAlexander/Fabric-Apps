# 04: paragliding-insights

*Post 4 of 15 in the Fabric Apps series. One per weekday.*

## Post text

```text
Nine by eight kilometres of the Allgäu Alps, at true scale, in a browser tab.

Paragliding Insights renders the airspace above Oberstdorf and the Nebelhorn from official 1 m elevation data, draws real flight tracks over it, and shows live glider traffic from the Open Glider Network.

Terrain is not decoration here. If the ridge is a metre off, the track flies through it.

Everything upstream is Fabric: notebooks derive the terrain tiles, pipelines schedule it, and the app is the delivery vehicle.

Terrain and buildings are Bavaria's, not mine:
Datenquelle: Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung – www.geodaten.bayern.de [Daten bearbeitet]
Live traffic: Open Glider Network (ODbL).

Source, and always the latest version:
https://github.com/KornAlexander/Fabric-Apps/tree/main/games-and-learn/paragliding-insights
```
