## Post text

```text
Live aircraft on approach, drawn over a 3D airport twin.

Airport IQ pulls live ADS-B from the public airplanes.live API and puts it over an airport model built from real OpenStreetMap gate geometry.

Two views behind one landing page: the approach, and what is happening on the ground.

Public feeds, public geometry, no tenant data. It deploys into your workspace and talks to nothing of mine.

Source, and always the latest version:
https://github.com/KornAlexander/Fabric-Apps/tree/main/industry/airport-iq
```
