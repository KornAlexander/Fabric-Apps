## Post text

```text
136 people died in the Ahr valley on 14 July 2021. This reconstructs what was known.

24.6 km of valley in 3D from official 1 m terrain and LoD2 buildings. The flood replays along a timeline driven by a stage-discharge rating built from real cross-sections. Everything from public data.

The simulated extent is scored against the Copernicus EMSR517 trace: IoU 0.508. That number is in the app, next to the map, including when it is unflattering.

Demonstration and training only. Not a risk assessment, and it says so before it says anything else.
```
