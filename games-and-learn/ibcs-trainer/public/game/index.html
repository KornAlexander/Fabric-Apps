Actionable Reporting · Learn by Playing

The IBCS Trainer Arcade

Three browser mini-games that teach the IBCS® SUCCESS rules for business charts — spot the violations, collect the compliant visuals and make better reports without reading a single standard. No install, runs right here.

🎮
Platformer

Rule Platformer

Run and jump through nine IBCS rule levels. Stomp the bad charts, collect the compliant ones and learn one SUCCESS rule per stage. Arrow keys / WASD + Space — or touch controls on mobile.

🔥
Quick reflex

Chart Swipe

Swipe right on the IBCS-compliant chart, left on the rule-breaker. A fast-paced way to train your eye for clean design. How many can you classify before the timer runs out?

🔐
Puzzle

IBCS Escape Room

Work through chart puzzles room by room. Pick the compliant visual to unlock the next door and escape — each room hides a different SUCCESS rule.

Built on Rayfin Apps in Microsoft Fabric · charts illustrate the IBCS® Standards.
Tip: click a game, then click the canvas once so keyboard & sound activate.
