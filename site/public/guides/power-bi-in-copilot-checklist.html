Good to know

Same permissionsCopilot uses each user's own Power BI access, including RLS and OLS.
Labels and DLPSensitivity labels carry over to the chat. Purview DLP can block labelled content.
Data freshnessAnswers reflect the last model refresh. Query data is processed in Fabric under the Fabric Product Terms.
